What we take on

The same work, pointed at your system.

Five things we take on, and one we don’t. If your problem isn’t in the first five, we’ll tell you on the first call rather than learn on your budget.

Real-time delivery to the browser

The layer between your data and your live interface: one authenticated connection, updates that keep their order, and reconnects that don’t lose or repeat what the user should have seen.

Event-driven backends

Multi-worker pipelines that handle each item once, survive a restart without redoing work, set aside whatever keeps failing, and come back cleanly from a crash mid-operation.

Payments, billing and invoicing

Checkout, subscription state, invoices and receipts — built so that a payment arriving twice, or a callback arriving late, still leaves exactly one correct outcome behind it.

Email that has to arrive

Password resets, receipts and statements engineered so a send is never lost and never doubled — and so that afterwards you can say exactly what went out, and when.

Market data & trading systems

The specialist lane: live feed ingestion, strategy scheduling, order execution, broker integrations, and cost modelling that matches what actually gets charged.

What we don’t take on

Front-end and design. We’d rather say so on the first call than subcontract it badly — and we’ll define a clean API contract so your front-end developer has an easy job of it.

How it goes

No discovery theatre.

You’ll know inside a week whether this is working, and so will we.

1

A call

Tell us what the system does and what’s going wrong. Free, no pitch. If we’re not the right pair for it we’ll say so, and point you somewhere better if we can.

2

A written plan

What we’ll do, what we won’t, what it costs and when it lands. Fixed scope for defined work; a retainer if it’s ongoing.

3

Weekly, in writing

Commits as they happen, and a short note every week: what moved, what’s next, what worries us. No month-long silences.

4

Handover

Tests, a README your next developer can actually follow, and a walkthrough. The point is that you don’t need us afterwards.

Contact

Tell us what you’re building.

One paragraph is plenty: what the system does, what’s going wrong or what’s missing, and roughly when you need it. You’ll get a straight answer about whether we can help and what we’d do first — no sales call required.
